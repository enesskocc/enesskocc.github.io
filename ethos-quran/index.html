A verse of the Quran on your home screen each day, with prayer times you can rely on. No advertising, no account, nothing about you leaves your phone.

What it does

  • A daily verse on the home screen and lock screen, in sixteen designs
  • Prayer times calculated on your device, with the method always shown
  • Prayer notifications scheduled in advance so they actually arrive
  • Qibla compass, a private prayer tracker and daily goals
  • English, Turkish, Arabic, French and German
  • Works fully in airplane mode
