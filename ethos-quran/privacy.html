Ethos Quran Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-16

Ethos Quran is built so that there is almost nothing to collect. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours that your data is sent to.

What stays on your device

All of it. Your settings, your chosen language and themes, your favourites, your prayer log, goals and make-up counts, and your location are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location

If you allow location access, your coordinates are used on your device to calculate prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. They are never uploaded, never shared, and never stored anywhere but your phone. You can instead pick a city by hand and not grant location access at all.

City names are matched against a list included in the app, so the app does not ask Apple, Google or anyone else to look your position up.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. The app contains no analytics, no attribution, no advertising and no third-party tracking software of any kind.

App updates

When the app opens, it asks Expo, the service that delivers small updates to the app between App Store releases, whether a newer version is available. That request carries only technical details needed to pick the right update, such as the app version and platform, and a random identifier created for this installation so updates can be delivered and counted. The identifier is not linked to you, and the request contains nothing about your location or your records.

Purchases

Ethos Quran Pro is an optional purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know whether your purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase receipt together with an anonymous identifier generated for the installation. It is not linked to your name, your email or your location. If you never purchase anything, none of this happens.

Quran text and city data

The Quranic text and its translations are bundled with the app and reproduced verbatim from published editions; the sources are listed inside the app under Settings. The offline city list is derived from GeoNames and used under CC BY 4.0.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data, so it holds nothing about children either.

Your rights

Because nothing personal leaves your device, there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase on request. You can remove everything the app holds by deleting it. If you have purchased Pro and want your RevenueCat record deleted, write to us and we will ask for its removal.

Changes

If a future version ever collects something, this page will say so before that version ships.
