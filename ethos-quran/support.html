Ethos Quran Privacy Support

Support

Questions, problems and requests: ethosquransupport@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you are using.

The widget shows the wrong day

Open the app once. It hands the widget a couple of weeks of content in advance, so it stays right offline, but that window is refreshed when you open the app.

Prayer times differ from my calendar

Different authorities use different methods. Open Prayer Times and check which method is shown at the bottom; Settings lets you change it. With the Diyanet method, Asr is given by the same rule for every school of thought, which is what the Diyanet calendar publishes.

A calculation and a printed calendar can still differ by a few minutes, and some authorities apply their own corrections that no formula reproduces exactly. Settings, Time corrections lets you shift any prayer by a minute or two so the app matches the calendar or the mosque you follow. The correction applies everywhere at once: the screen, the widget and the notifications.

Notifications do not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Ethos Quran in the iOS Settings app, and that the prayer you expect is switched on in the app under Settings.

The Qibla points the wrong way

Hold the phone flat and away from metal and magnets, and move it in a figure eight to calibrate the compass. Allowing location access lets the app use true north instead of magnetic north, which is a few degrees more accurate.

I paid and Pro is not active

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Managing a subscription

Subscriptions are managed in the App Store, not in the app: iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
